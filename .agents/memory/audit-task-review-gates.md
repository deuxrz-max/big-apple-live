description: Owner's required stop-and-review workflow for the numbered cleanup list.

Work on one numbered audit cleanup task at a time, starting with Task 1. After completing each task, stop and report exactly what changed and how to check it. Do not start the next task until the owner has reviewed it and explicitly said to continue.

**Why:** The owner explicitly requires a review between every task, not just after Task 1.

**How to apply:** Preserve a pre-change rollback snapshot with honest coverage limitations. Do not publish; leave Buy Coins Pay disabled and all card-game switches off.
