Deux Wild rooms should have solid deep-black background/panel surfaces and a thin, softly glowing goldish perimeter. Gold is edge lighting, not a gold room fill. Keep readable contrasting controls and preserve existing seating, photos, gift artwork, videos and functionality. The original room-only styling scope is superseded by the owner's later [app-wide mobile redesign approval](app-visual-direction.md); the room's black surfaces and media/audio requirements remain.

The owner reiterated “Everything's black”: all room interface surface fills, including empty-seat circles, buttons, boxes and room gift-sheet controls, must use the same black rather than mixed gray/purple fills. The gold highlight should travel smoothly and continuously around the outer phone-screen-shaped rectangle and corners only, not sweep through content. No lightning, jagged electricity, strobing or confetti.

**Why:** The owner explicitly requested a color-and-edge-lighting-only match to a black image with a luminous gold frame, not its clown/cracked-glass artwork or a redesign, and corrected the initial mixed-fill/static-frame treatment.

**How to apply:** Scope visual changes to rooms, including room-only portalled sheets; keep decorative framing non-interactive and verify it does not alter layout or obstruct controls. Do not recolor or filter media. Keep a static frame for reduced motion.
