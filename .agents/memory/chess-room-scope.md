description: Product boundary for two-player Chess rooms versus regular social, TV and card-game rooms.
---

Chess is a separate audio-only room type opened through the Go Live / Create Room menu. It has exactly two player boxes (White above the board, Black below), no extra seats, no audience, no chat or chat input, no TV and no Deux Wild card game.

**Why:** The user explicitly requested “2 boxes only. No other seats and no chat,” while preserving every existing room type and the hidden card-game settings.

**How to apply:** Do not treat Chess as an ordinary nine-seat created room or attach gifting/card-game/TV/chat components to it. Keep existing room types unchanged. Chess awards no coins or rewards.

Chess's two players should use large, edge-to-edge photo sections above and below the full-width square board, with independently swipeable profile-photo collections, Me-page-style position dots, and identity overlays nearest the board. Preserve whole uncropped photos and sharp thin gold frames; do not restore compact avatar rows.

**Why:** The owner explicitly requested “Pics should take the whole top and bottom and leave the board in the middle,” building on the full-photo/no-oval rule.

**How to apply:** Use the screen's remaining space for photos with no layout gaps, keep swiping isolated from board moves, and retain approved-photo visibility. This visual request does not itself add a spectator access mode.
