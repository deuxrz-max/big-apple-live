---
name: Chess room scope
description: Product boundary for two-player Chess rooms versus regular social, TV and card-game rooms.
---

Chess is a separate audio-only room type opened through the Go Live / Create Room menu. It has exactly two player boxes (White above the board, Black below), no extra seats, no audience, no chat or chat input, no TV and no Deux Wild card game.

**Why:** The user explicitly requested “2 boxes only. No other seats and no chat,” while preserving every existing room type and the hidden card-game settings.

**How to apply:** Do not treat Chess as an ordinary nine-seat created room or attach gifting/card-game/TV/chat components to it. Keep existing room types unchanged. Chess awards no coins or rewards.
