---
name: Profile picture navigation
description: Direct picture-to-profile navigation and protected room/Chess interactions.
---
Tapping a real user's picture should immediately open their profile, without an intervening action menu or confirmation. Closing the quick profile must leave the current screen and its state intact; offer a clear full-profile link and back path.

**Why:** The owner explicitly requested simple, self-explanatory picture navigation throughout Deux Wild.

**How to apply:** Keep photo actions separate from joining rooms, selecting gift recipients, opening message threads and toggling microphones. Preserve swipe gestures on Chess and profile photo carousels. Created-room profile popups retain profile information and the current hand; do not introduce cards into other room types. Editing/reviewing uploaded photos remains an editing/review action rather than profile navigation.
