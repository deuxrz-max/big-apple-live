---
name: Photo retention approval
description: User-approved grace period and limits on irreversible photo cleanup.
---

Unused profile uploads normally retain a 30-day grace period. The owner's newer single-photo profile requirement explicitly authorizes immediate deletion of a saved photo when replaced or removed; do not apply that exception to unrelated uploads.
Existing unused photos receive a fresh grace period when retention is introduced.
Currently referenced images and concurrent saves must remain protected; cleanup
must never reset storage or delete account, financial, or message data.

**Why:** The user chose 30 days when asked to approve irreversible deletion on
2026-10-07; historical uploads had intentionally been retained.

**How to apply:** Preserve this approved policy in subsequent maintenance changes.
The single-photo replacement exception applies only to that account's superseded saved photo. Ask before expanding cleanup beyond this scope.
