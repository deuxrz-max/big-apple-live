---
name: Race-locked home cities
description: Home-city ownership and display rules for player and boss cards.
---
Players inherit a locked home city from their race; Humans belong to Oakhaven (The Crossroads). Players do not choose or override it. This applies to battle, room-seat and profile cards. Bosses show their actual city of origin.

**Why:** the owner explicitly requested race-assigned origins rather than a player-editable home city.

**How to apply:** extend the shared race mapping when adding races. A future second city must come from real faction ownership data; never infer faction control from origin or display an unheld city.
