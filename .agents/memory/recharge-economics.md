name: Recharge economics safeguards
description: Owner's broadcaster conversion, recharge proposal boundaries and unknown-fee safeguards.
---

The owner specifies three broadcaster-earned units per US cent, with a 30,000-earned-unit ($100) cash-out minimum. This supersedes the older $53.97 setting. It is not guaranteed income or a monthly payout schedule. Purchased coins, including purchase bonuses, must not be directly cash-redeemable.

All recharge coins, including bonuses, are potential broadcaster payout liability. Include the full issued amount in economics, and subtract applicable percentage fees, fixed fees and other known transaction costs before discussing remaining margin. Unknown fees are not zero; illustrative fees do not establish profitability.

Bonus quantities are proposals until explicitly approved. Keep real checkout off until the owner approves the schedule and fees are verified. Do not automatically treat an approval of the screen design as approval of the bonus economics.

**Why:** The owner expressly required conservative proposals, approval before enabling bonuses, and no profit guarantees.

**How to apply:** Keep spendable purchase funds distinct from verified earned/redeemable funds. The legacy spendable wallet and historical earned/received counters do not establish a redeemable balance. Do not migrate or reclassify those balances silently; separate accounting needs an explicit, audited implementation before real payout integration.
