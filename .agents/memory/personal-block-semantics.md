---
name: Personal block boundaries
description: Deux Wild personal blocks are communication filters, not room bans.
---

A personal block stops direct messages and room whispers in both directions, and keeps the existing filtering of public room and livestream text for the blocking viewer. It must not remove either person from a third party's room, hide their presence or profile photo there, mute their voice, unseat them, or suppress their gift effects. Host kicks and host-wide blocks remain separate controls.

**Why:** The user explicitly distinguishes personal communication boundaries from host moderation and requires both blocked people to remain visible and audible to everyone in a third party's room.

**How to apply:** When changing personal-block behavior, retain text filtering and bilateral DM/whisper restrictions while leaving room membership, seating, voice, video, and gift-event delivery unaffected.
