---
name: Audio beam constraints
description: Owner-approved transport and privacy constraints for Streamtalk university beams.
---

University beams are audio-only: no camera capture or camera permission. Prefer direct peer-to-peer audio with free connection helpers; do not add hosted or paid live-media services. If restrictive networks make a relay necessary, propose a self-hosted option and explain the tradeoffs before adding it.

**Why:** the owner changed the beam plan from video to audio-only and explicitly rejected a service that could create future billing.

**How to apply:** preserve the four-speaker-slot limit and use direct connections by default; clearly disclose that some NAT/firewall combinations may fail without TURN and that P2P host-mute relies on clients honoring the server's moderation event.
