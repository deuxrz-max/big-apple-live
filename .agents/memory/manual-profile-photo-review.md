---
name: Manual profile-photo review
description: User preference for human review rather than automatic AI moderation of profile photos.
---

New profile photos should remain pending for human review. Do not enable automatic AI image checks or send uploaded profile photos to an external moderation service without the user's renewed approval.

**Why:** the user chose manual review instead of enabling AI image checks.

**How to apply:** keep the pending-photo review flow available to authorized reviewers; approve or reject photos manually.
