The owner approved replacing the previous seven gifting tiers and colored logo with numbered shield levels 1–100, not showing both systems alongside each other. The first successful gift unlocks Level 1. Progress is successful outgoing gift coin value, one coin = one XP, including quantity sends once at their full value.

The tier perks are cumulative: Bronze has the badge only. Silver, Gold, Platinum and Deux Wild each unlock a name color and an animated frame matching that tier's color. Gold's entrance banner remains inherited by higher tiers; Deux Wild keeps its neon full-screen entrance. The owner removed the Platinum entrance effect and Platinum-exclusive gifts completely. Neon green is reserved for the Deux Wild gifting tier. Keep legacy purchase-based username-color accounting and monthly purchase badges separate; the Silver+ name-color perk must not erase a purchased color.

**Why:** The owner chose replacement with numbered shields, then explicitly revised the rewards so every Silver+ tier gets both perks and Platinum no longer has a special entrance effect or exclusive gifts.

**How to apply:** Exclude recharges, received gifts, rewards, inventory purchases, rejected or rolled-back sends, duplicate retries and other accounts. Do not restore Platinum gift locks or their Studio toggle; old stored flags must be inert. Appearance-only development overrides must never change wallets/history.
