SUMMER BACKGROUND LOOP — DIAGNOSIS AND VERIFICATION

Only the summer media files were replaced. Application source code, the
40% black tint, full-screen coverage, and dark-only fallback were unchanged.
Nothing was published or deployed.

METHOD
ffprobe stream/frame inspection; ffmpeg decoded-frame comparison.
Image metric: mean absolute difference (MAD), 0–255 grayscale levels.
For comparable framing, images were center-cropped/scaled to 400x736,
then measured at 200x368. Neighbor measurements use each clip's native FPS.
Browser timings use requestVideoFrameCallback in workspace Chromium.
These are real decoded/displayed frame measurements, not mocked playback.
