Autoplay

Code inspection shows the YouTube player requests autoplay, mute and inline
playback. If the player remains unstarted or paused after its check, the UI can
show a "Tap to play" fallback. Therefore muted autoplay is intended to start
without a tap, but a tap may be needed in a browser that blocks it. The separate
"Tap anywhere for sound" hint enables sound and is not the playback-start
fallback.
